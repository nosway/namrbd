# Code Of Conduct

NAMRBD follows a simple contribution standard: be respectful, assume good
intent, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, personal attacks, or
publishing private information without consent. Maintainers may moderate issues,
pull requests, discussions, or community spaces to keep collaboration useful and
safe.

Report conduct concerns to taewoong.kim@gmail.com.
